Heat / Pop Dice
Heat or Pop Dice are determined by your player Attitude
-Player Attitude is choosing Face or Heel
- Faces get Pop Dice
- Heels get Heat Dice

Heat / Pop Dice are gained during
- Promo’s
- Some Common Moves
- All Legendary Moves
Heat / Pop Dice can be added to any roll
Heat / Pop Dice can be spent at any time on your turn to gain +10 Stamina

Promo’s
Promos happen before a match and give a wrestler the time to amp up the crowd and, state their goals for the match, and
gain some additional heat pop dice
Promo’s use 3 Big Gold Dice
each dice roll has the ability to gain 1 Heat/Pop dice
The wrestler must roll at or below their level to receive a Heat / Pop dice

Pin/Submission Mechanics
If a move taker body position ends prone, the move maker can attempt a Pin
A pin consists of 3 head to head rolls of the BGD
Each wrestler rolls the BGD
Pinning
The pinning wrestler subtracts their core body points
The pinned wrestler subtracts all of their body points
Submission
The (L) wrestler subtracts their body points associated with the move assignment
Kickout/Submission Escape
If the pinned wrestler wins any roll, then the match is neurtral neutral with both players prone.
The previously pinned wrestler now gets to make a move
The previously pinned character gains +10 Stamina
If the pinned wrestler does not win any of the three rolls the match is over
Finisher Move to Pin
-Every Finisher move can immediately go into a pin roll mechanic on success

Stamina
Stamina is the main energy of each player
Moves cost stamina to make
Moves take away stamina from the targeted player
If a player reaches 0 Stamina, the (H) wrestler must make a pin or submission move
Players regain +10 Stamina during every move at turn starting at neutral position
Both players gain stamina on (N/N) rolls

Leveling Up
Hitting The Gym
Hitting the gym allows the wrestler to add to their Core Stats
Players get two +1’s to add to any Core Stat
Hitting The Mat
Hitting The Mat is adding moves and increasing body part threshold / Making it harder to get hit
Players get to add 1 legendary move from their style

Players get one +1 to increase the threshold for any body part
Limits
The user has 10 Gym Passes and 10 Mat Passes.
Each can be used at any time and in any order
