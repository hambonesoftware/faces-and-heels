5. What is the missing **body requirement modifier** rule?
6. What are the exact **stamina costs and target stamina loss** rules?
7. What size are **Heat/Pop Dice**, how many can be held/spent, and when are they spent?
8. What is the complete **position vocabulary** and legal bonus repositioning?
9. What is the complete **submission procedure**?
10. What is the canonical **move list/data** for the first playable version?
