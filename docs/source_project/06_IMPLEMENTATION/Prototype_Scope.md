# Prototype Scope

## Goal

Prove that a touch-first match is fun and readable before building campaign systems or a large art catalog.

## Prototype should include

### Characters

- 2 test wrestlers.
- Face/Heel distinction.
- enough stats to exercise the rules engine.

### Match systems

- match start / Neutral flow;
- H/N/L tracking once rules are approved;
- legal-move filtering;
- stamina spending/recovery;
- targeted body part and threshold checks;
- Reaction opportunity;
- Pop/Heat spend;
- prone state;
- Pin sequence;
- match completion.

### Moves

Use a deliberately small, non-canon test catalog until the creator supplies/approves the full move list:

- 1 basic strike;
- 2 grapples;
- 1 grounded move;
- 1 rope/Match-Level move;
- 1 Legendary move;
- 1 Finisher.

### Graphics

- ring;
- 2 wrestler templates;
- idle / prone / get-up;
- 3–4 test paired move animations;
- pin and kickout;
- impact effects;
- compact condition UI.

### PWA

- installable;
- landscape match mode;
- portrait menus;
- local save;
- works after refresh;
- basic offline shell.

## Excluded from first prototype

- large move catalog;
- full career mode;
- championships;
- AI-generated dynamic commentary;
- online leagues;
- complex creator marketplace;
- full Discord Activity integration;
- 3D rendering;
- dozens of cosmetic items;
- elaborate crowd simulation.
