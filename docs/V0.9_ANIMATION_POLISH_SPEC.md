# Faces & Heels — v0.9 Animation Polish Specification

**Build focus:** physical contact, paired animation readability, and impact feel.

## Goals

1. Attacker and defender must visibly occupy the same wrestling action rather than looking like two unrelated sprites playing near each other.
2. Every contact move uses an authored pair choreography: sprite pose timeline + root-motion timeline + impact marker.
3. Impact feedback is layered at runtime so it does not need to be baked into every sprite atlas.
4. Existing Smart Flow and HUD specifications remain unchanged and authoritative.

## Paired-animation contract

Each paired move supplies:

- attacker atlas;
- defender atlas;
- shared duration/fps;
- impact frame;
- attacker root-motion track;
- defender root-motion track;
- optional hit-stop duration;
- impact point;
- impact class: strike, grapple, slam, or kickout.

Root motion is normalized to arena coordinates so the same atlas can be reused across responsive screen sizes.

## v0.9 choreography

### Forearm Smash

- attacker steps into range during the windup;
- defender closes enough for visible forearm/head contact;
- 72 ms hit stop;
- defender recoils away after impact;
- strike rays and light particles appear at the head contact point.

### Vertical Suplex

- approach → waist/upper-body lock → crouch → lift → inverted hold → bridge/slam → recovery;
- defender root rises during the lift rather than staying anchored to the mat;
- defender overlaps attacker during the vertical phase;
- 92 ms hit stop at mat impact;
- stronger camera shake, mat compression, radial impact and dust particles;
- defender resolves to prone.

### Dragon Screw

- attacker closes low and captures the leg;
- defender leg lifts before the twist;
- both roots rotate/fall into the takedown;
- 82 ms hit stop on landing;
- defender resolves to prone.

### Ground Headlock

- only available against a prone defender;
- attacker kneels beside the defender;
- roots close into a persistent hold;
- short 55 ms cinch impact;
- attacker is drawn over defender for readable control.

### Pin / Kickout

- pin pose keeps attacker centered over defender;
- kickout creates a body burst and separation;
- 65 ms kickout hit stop;
- gold impact feedback distinguishes escape from offensive impact.

## Runtime presentation

The Canvas 2D renderer remains on `requestAnimationFrame()`. Sprite artwork runs at authored 8–16 fps depending on state. Root motion is interpolated every display frame, which makes the low-frame-count character art feel much smoother without requiring more rendered sprite frames.

Runtime effects:

- hit stop;
- camera-scale pulse;
- impact rays;
- procedural particles;
- arena shake;
- ring/mat bounce.

## Acceptance criteria

- Forearm Smash visibly reaches the defender's head at impact.
- Vertical Suplex defender visibly leaves the mat and becomes inverted.
- Vertical Suplex ends with the defender prone rather than snapping back upright.
- Dragon Screw visibly captures the defender's leg before the takedown.
- Ground Headlock positions the attacker beside/over the prone defender.
- Impact events fire once per move.
- Hit stop freezes both attacker and defender on the contact frame.
- Existing Smart Flow tests continue to pass.
- No external image assets are required for wrestler art.
